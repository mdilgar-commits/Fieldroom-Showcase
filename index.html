01 / PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Closeout work,
organized by room.

Fieldroom is a construction room matrix workspace that brings project deliverables, source references, and field progress together in one place.

Project teams often have to connect requirements scattered across drawings and documents with the real rooms where work happens. Fieldroom helps organize those requirements by room and trade, then gives the team a practical way to review, update, and follow through.

AI can help find the signal.
People make the call.

When enabled, Gemini reads supported project documents and proposes room labels or deliverable candidates. Each suggestion stays in a pre-check review step so a person can verify the room, trade, work description, and source before adding it to the matrix.

✳ AI-assisted, human-verified

Suggestions are not validated against project requirements automatically. Teams should compare each item with its cited source.
